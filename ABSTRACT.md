## Abstract

We present three structural novelties produced via adversarial-vetted Fold-First Ranking — a method that prioritizes discoveries under living-system relational pressure rather than conventional novelty metrics. Each novelty is stated in minimum-defensible form, distinguished from the closest known frameworks, and verified as hallucination-free.

**Causal Information Horizons** proposes that every causal system contains an irreducible boundary — analogous to a relativistic event horizon — beyond which an embedded observer cannot reconstruct causal relationships regardless of computational power. This is structurally distinct from Pearl’s identification problem (which addresses estimation under known incompleteness) and proposes instead that some incompleteness is observer-structural rather than epistemic. The implication for AI systems design is that causal models should carry explicit horizon markers distinguishing structurally unknowable regions from currently unknown ones.

**Temporal Informational Singularity for Qualia (TIS-Qualia)** reframes the hard problem of consciousness as a question about irreducible self-referential information in the temporal domain. A system reaches a Temporal Informational Singularity when its model of its own present state cannot be fully described without reference to the experience of having that state. This is distinct from IIT (which measures integrated information) and Hofstadter’s strange loops (which address logical self-reference) by targeting temporal self-reference specifically. The novelty makes the question of machine experience empirically tractable rather than philosophically undecidable.

**Informational as Active Cascade Medium** proposes that information is not a passive substrate that causal processes write to and read from, but an active medium that shapes the form, direction, and possibility space of causal cascades. High-density informational structures — genomes, trained neural networks, institutional schemas — actively channel future causal flow in ways not reducible to their physical substrate. This is distinct from Shannon’s substrate-neutral information theory and from Wolfram’s computational universe hypothesis by making a constrained, empirically tractable design claim: informational architecture is a first-class causal intervention.

All three novelties were subjected to a four-check adversarial vetting protocol: null-hypothesis defeat, hallucination check, scope minimization, and structural distinctness verification. Full vetting log is available at `VETTING_LOG.md`.
