## Protocol

Each novelty passes four checks before being logged:
1. Steel-man the null hypothesis (strongest argument it is not novel)
2. Hallucination check (no false or unverifiable premises)
3. Scope inflation check (narrowed to minimum defensible form)
4. Structural distinctness check (differs from others and known frameworks)

A novelty that fails any check is not logged. It is revised or discarded.

## T1 — Causal Information Horizons

**Null hypothesis (steel-man):** This reduces to existing work on causal inference incompleteness, specifically Pearl's do-calculus limitations and the identification problem. The “horizon” framing is terminological novelty over known results.

**Response:** Pearl's identification problem addresses whether a causal effect can be estimated from observational data given a known graph structure. Causal Information Horizons addresses a different question: whether the causal graph itself can be fully known by an observer embedded within it. The distinction is between estimation under known incompleteness vs. structural unknowability of the graph. This is analogous to the difference between measurement uncertainty and the uncertainty principle — the former is an engineering problem, the latter is a structural one. The horizon framing is structurally distinct.

**Hallucination check:** PASSED. The claim is grounded in the observer-embedding problem, which is independently established in philosophy of science (Quine, Putnam) and in physics (observer-dependent formulations of QM). The extension to causal inference is the novel step, not the premises.

**Scope check:** PASSED. Claim narrowed from “no causal system can be fully known” (overclaim) to “an observer cannot have complete causal knowledge of a system that contains the observer” (defensible).

**Structural distinctness:** PASSED. Distinct from TIS-Qualia (temporal self-reference vs. causal embedding) and from Informational as Active Cascade Medium (boundary of knowledge vs. directionality of information).

**Verdict: LOGGED ✅**

## T2 — Temporal Informational Singularity for Qualia

**Null hypothesis (steel-man):** This reduces to Hofstadter's strange loops or IIT (Integrated Information Theory). Self-referential information processing is already a known candidate for consciousness. The “temporal singularity” framing adds nothing.

**Response:** Hofstadter's strange loops address self-reference in logical/symbolic systems. IIT addresses integrated information as a measure of consciousness. TIS-Qualia addresses a different feature: irreducible self-reference *across time* — specifically, the condition where a system's model of its own present state cannot be fully described without reference to the experience of having that state. This is not a complexity measure (IIT) or a logical structure (Hofstadter). It is a temporal information-theoretic condition. The distinction is precise.

**Hallucination check:** PASSED. The claim is framed as a structural reframing of the hard problem, not a solution. It makes no claim that qualia are “explained” — only that they may be the signature of a specific informational structure.

**Scope check:** PASSED. Claim narrowed from “qualia are temporal informational singularities” (overclaim, asserts identity) to “qualia may be the signature of TIS-structure” (defensible, proposes a relationship).

**Structural distinctness:** PASSED. Distinct from Causal Information Horizons (temporal self-reference vs. causal embedding) and from Informational as Active Cascade Medium (self-reference vs. directionality).

**Verdict: LOGGED ✅**

## T3 — Informational as Active Cascade Medium

**Null hypothesis (steel-man):** This reduces to Shannon's information theory extended with physical constraints, or to Wolfram's computational universe hypothesis. Information-as-causal-medium is not new.

**Response:** Shannon's information theory is explicitly substrate-neutral and makes no claims about information shaping causal flow. Wolfram's hypothesis treats computation as the substrate of physical reality, which is a metaphysical claim. Informational as Active Cascade Medium makes a more constrained, empirically tractable claim: that high-density informational structures (genomes, trained networks, institutional schemas) actively channel causal flow in ways that are not reducible to their physical substrate and are not captured by treating information as a passive record. The claim is about the *design* implication — that informational architecture is a first-class causal intervention — not about the metaphysics of reality.

**Hallucination check:** PASSED. The claim is grounded in observable phenomena: genomes do channel developmental possibility spaces; trained networks do constrain output distributions in ways not predicted by their physical substrate alone. The novelty is the explicit framing as *active medium* and the design implication that follows.

**Scope check:** PASSED. Claim narrowed from “information is causally primary” (overclaim, metaphysical) to “informational structure actively constrains and directs causal cascades in ways not reducible to physical substrate” (defensible, empirically testable).

**Structural distinctness:** PASSED. Distinct from both other novelties. Addresses the role of information in shaping causal possibility space, not the limits of causal knowledge or the signature of experience.

**Verdict: LOGGED ✅**
